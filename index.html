About Us

Born in Italy.
Racing in the Gulf.

ItalCorse Middle East is the regional racing team of ItalCorse, the Italian kart manufacturer. We bring factory chassis, Italian race preparation and professional coaching to drivers across the UAE and the wider region.

From first-time racers to championship contenders, every driver gets the same standard: a kart prepared properly, a clear plan, and a team behind them on every race weekend.

Meet the Team

The people behind the karts

Our team combines experienced mechanics, dedicated coaches and drivers racing across several categories. Everyone works together from the workshop to the grid.

Each driver has a mechanic preparing their kart and a coach reviewing their data after every session. That is how we keep improving, race after race.
