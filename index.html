About Us

Italian DNA.
Gulf speed.

ItalCorse Middle East is the regional race team of ItalCorse, the Italian kart manufacturer. We bring factory chassis, Italian race preparation and professional coaching to drivers across the UAE.

From a driver's first race to a championship campaign, everyone gets the same standard: a properly prepared kart, a clear plan and a full team behind them on every race weekend.
